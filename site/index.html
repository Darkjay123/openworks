Fund projects.
Pay only for verified work.

OpenWorks is a milestone treasury for anyone who funds projects: grant programs, NGOs, companies paying vendors, public bodies. A council approves spending with on-chain multisig. Each project gets its own vault. Money moves one milestone at a time, after independent reviewers sign off, and unspent funds can only return to the treasury.

03 · Where funding leaks, and what the contracts enforce

Rules in code, not in policy

⚖︎

No single approver

M-of-N council signatures, then a timelock that starts when the threshold is reached. Anyone can execute once it passes.

✓

Verify before paying

k-of-n independent reviewers certify each milestone, strictly in order. The recipient can never review their own work.

◷

Public challenge window

After certification there is a window in which the council can void it. The contract refuses to create a project whose window is shorter than the council's timelock.

⛨

Retention for defects

A share of each payment is held until a defects-liability period ends. The council can forfeit it for defects inside that period.

⇄

No way to divert funds

There is no "send to address" action. Treasury money can only enter a vault; a vault can only pay its recipient or refund the treasury.

↺

Cancel means refund

Cancelling a project returns every unspent unit to the treasury in the same transaction. Earned retention stays owed.
